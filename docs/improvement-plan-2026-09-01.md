# Musallah — Code review and improvement plan (2026-09-01)

Scope: every file in `app/`, `src/`, `scripts/`, the workflow, docs and repo root, at commit `3f08b90` (0.1.8). Baseline: `npm test` 337/337 green, `npm run typecheck` clean.

> **STATUS — executed 2026-09-01, the same day.** Seven commits on `main`, `b66c997` through `c861584`, not pushed. Every A, B, C and D ticket is done, with three deviations: **C12** became a `floatingEdge` helper rather than a level on `cardEdge`, because the three hand-rolled sites keep their Android elevation in dark mode and `cardEdge` deliberately does not; **C19** was done selectively (107 comment lines removed, not 800 — most of what remains is load-bearing rationale); **A5/D7** were corrected mid-way (`coord-report.md` was untracked and ignored all along, so four files were removed, not five). Tests 337 → 333 (the two dead-code tests went with their code), typecheck clean, `expo export --platform android` builds. **The on-device checks in phases 2–4 are still pending — no phone was attached.**
>
> **Bug found on the way, by C11:** three quick-filter chips used keys the schema does not have (`womens_area`, `wheelchair_accessible`, `wudu_facilities`), so tapping Sisters' space, Disabled access or Wudu filtered out every place. Fixed in `6389917` by deriving the chips from the schema's own labels and icons.
>
> **Measured, not estimated:** `app/` + `src/` (non-test) went from 14,508 to 14,311 lines and from 2,888 to 2,781 comment lines — a net −197, well short of the −1,500 to −2,000 written below, because the file splits and the new hooks each add imports, prop types and a header. The repo as a whole lost 8,650 lines net (the tracked artefacts and the two dead scripts). What actually changed is structure: seven mechanisms that were written out three or four times each now exist once, two unshipped features and their tests are gone, the two 1,000+ line screens are 736 and 600 lines, and six fragility fixes landed.

The brief was optimisation so every feature keeps working, removal of anything obsolete or pointless, and shortening code that is long-winded for no reason. This plan is the result. It supersedes `docs/improvement-plan-2026-08-07.md`, whose still-open items (typography migration, bottom-sheet translateY rewrite) are carried forward in section F.

**Verdict in one paragraph.** The app code is in good shape: the astronomy, the map pool, the sheets and the pipeline are all deliberately engineered and tested, and nothing found here is a user-facing breakage. What has accumulated is (a) dead code from features that were started and not shipped, (b) about 400 KB of tracked one-off audit output at the repo root, (c) comments and docs that still describe a bundled offline dataset the app stopped shipping in August, and (d) the same six or seven small mechanisms written out by hand in three or four places each. Fixing all of it removes roughly 1,500–2,000 lines from `app/` and `src/` and 2,400 lines of tracked artefacts, with no change in behaviour.

## C. Same outcome, less code

Each of these produces byte-identical behaviour. Ordered by how much it removes.

### C1 — One time formatter
`String(d.getHours()).padStart(2, "0")` is written out at **11 sites**: six in `prayerTimes.ts` alone (`format`, `make.display`, two `formatHm`), two in `qibla.tsx`, two in `jamaatContribution.ts`, one in `notificationPlan.ts`. Add `hhmm(date)` and `isoDate(date)` to `src/lib/duration.ts` (rename it `time.ts`) and use them everywhere. `place/[id].tsx` also hand-parses `YYYY-MM-DD` twice (`jamaatAgeDays`, `describeRecordedOn`) — the second should call the first, not re-run the regex.

### C2 — One five-prayer list
The literal `["fajr","dhuhr","asr","maghrib","isha"]` exists as `PRAYER_KEYS` (notificationPlan), `JAMAAT_KEYS` (jamaatContribution), `JAMAAT_PRAYER_KEYS` (placesRepo) and `PRAYER_ROWS` (place/[id]); `JamaatTimes` spells the five keys out again. One `PRAYER_KEYS` + `PRAYER_LABELS` in `src/data/places.ts` (they are the schema's vocabulary), and `JamaatTimes = Partial<Record<PrayerKey, string>> & { source; recordedOn }`.

### C3 — Drop `computePrayerTimes` and the capitalised `PrayerTimes` shape
`computePrayerTimes` (returns `{ Fajr, Sunrise, ... }`) has exactly one consumer, `place/[id].tsx`, which then needs `PRAYER_ROWS[].calculatedKey` to map lowercase jamaat keys onto capitalised calculated keys, and a `parseMinutes` helper to turn the "HH:MM" strings back into minutes. Switch the screen to `computePrayerSchedule` (keyed entries with real `Date`s): the mapping table, the string re-parsing and the second public API all disappear. One test `describe` adjusts.

### C4 — `useMinuteTick()` hook
The "sleep to the next minute boundary, and resync on AppState active" effect is copy-pasted into `index.tsx` (`NextPrayerBar`), `prayer.tsx` and `place/[id].tsx`. `qibla.tsx` does its own thing: a plain 60 s interval plus a `dayKey` memo keyed on the tick, which needed **three** `eslint-disable` comments to explain. One hook returning `now`; qibla derives `dayKey` from it and the eslint-disables go.

### C5 — `useDeviceLocation()` hook
Three copies of "request/read permission → `getLastKnownPositionAsync` → `getCurrentPositionAsync` → fallback to central London": `index.tsx` (prompts, then watches), `qibla.tsx` (prompts, no watch, has a retry path), `prayer.tsx` (never prompts). One hook with `{ prompt, watch }` options returning `{ coords, usingFallback, permission, retry }`. Keeps every current behaviour including the qibla retry and the home screen's onboarding gate. Roughly 120 lines → 50.

### C6 — `calcOptions` from the settings context
The memo `{ method, madhab, shafaq }` keyed on those three settings is in `index.tsx`, `prayer.tsx`, `place/[id].tsx` (inline) and `NotificationsContext.tsx`. Expose it once from `SettingsContext` as `calcOptions`.

### C7 — `OverlaySheet` component
`FilterSheet` and `SuggestionSheet` each carry ~70 identical lines: `useSheetAnimation`, `BackHandler` effect, `usePreventRemove`, `useOverlayLock`, the backdrop `Animated.View` with `opacity: progress` and `pointerEvents` toggling, `accessibilityViewIsModal` + `onAccessibilityEscape`, and the hidden scrim `Touchable`. `Onboarding` carries the lock/backdrop half. Extract `OverlaySheet({ visible, onClose, anchor: "top" | "bottom", children })`. While there, the two scrim alphas (`0.4` vs `0.45`) become one `scrim` colour token.

### C8 — `CheckRow` in FilterSheet
Three checkbox rows with the identical 26 pt box + `check-bold` markup. One sub-component.

### C9 — Data-driven contact rows
`place/[id].tsx` builds `contactRows` with four near-identical `if (place.x) contactRows.push({...})` blocks. A `CONTACT_FIELDS` table + `flatMap`. Also `openDirections` rebuilds the exact query string `mapsSearchUrl` already builds — call it.

### C10 — One distance-measuring closure on the home screen
`byDistance` and `favourites` both contain the same eight lines (`measureFromAnchor`, `sameAnchor`, `measureFromUser`, the `Result` construction). A single `useMemo`'d `measure(place): Result` shared by both.

### C11 — Type the quick filters
`QUICK_FILTERS[].key` is `string`, forcing two `key as FacilityKey` casts. Make it `FacilityKey | "saved"`.

### C12 — `cardEdge` takes a level
Three places hand-roll "hairline in dark, shadow in light" instead of using `cardEdge`: `index.tsx` `recenterButton`, `Onboarding` `card`, `SuggestionSheet` `card`; `qibla.tsx` has a fourth (`lift`) for the ambient level. Give `cardEdge(scheme, colors, level = "raised")` the elevation level and use it at all four. **Preserve the same-keys-in-both-schemes rule** — it is what prevents the Fabric prop-swap corruption on theme flip.

### C13 — `clipRipple` constant
`...Platform.select({ android: { overflow: "hidden" as const } })` appears 7 times with the same three-line comment each time. One exported constant in `elevation.ts` with the comment once.

### C14 — One `ThanksRow` in JamaatCheck
Confirmed / noted / outdated render three copies of the same icon+text row differing only in the trailing action.

### C15 — Prayer screen tidy
- `SunTrajectoryArc` calls `schedule.find` six times for three entries. Build a `byKey` map once.
- `selectedDate = addDays(now, dayOffset)` is a fresh `Date` every minute, so `schedule` and `dayPills` recompute every minute. Key them on the day, not the instant.
- `getPrayerStatus` should return `nextKey`; `isUpcoming` currently does `entry.key === status.nextLabel.toLowerCase()` — the label→key coupling that commit `8dfb116` said it removed is still there for the *next* prayer.

### C16 — Settings small stuff
Local `type IconName` duplicates the one exported from `src/lib/icons.ts`. `toggleNotifications` is a four-line wrapper around `on ? enable() : disable()`.

### C17 — Split the two 1,000+ line screens along seams that already exist
Pure moves, no logic change. The module-level themed-style cache means extraction costs nothing.
- `app/qibla.tsx` (1,633 lines) → `components/qibla/Dial.tsx`, `TurnTape.tsx`, `SunCard.tsx`, and a `useCompassHeading()` hook holding the sensor subscription + smoothing + animated values.
- `app/index.tsx` (1,472 lines) → `SearchBar`, `QuickFilterStrip`, `MapLegend`, `FridayBanner`, `EmptyState`. The five inline JSX variables are already exactly these components.

### C18 — Pipeline: one `toHHMM`
`isValidHHMM` is defined in `placesRepo.ts`, `sync-places.mjs`, `harvest-mawaqit.mjs` (and `csv-to-places.mjs`, going in A3); `toHHMM` in `lib/timetable.mjs` does the same job. Every `.mjs` should import `toHHMM`. The `.ts`/`.mjs` split itself has to stay (no build step for scripts) — but see D8.

### C19 — Comment density
2,888 of 14,508 non-test lines in `app/` + `src/` are comments (20%). The "why" comments are the codebase's best feature and must stay. What can go is the retelling: the `MIN_DIAL` negative-radius story is told in full three times (`qibla.tsx` twice, `qibla.ts` once, plus a test that pins it); the Fabric prop-swap warning is repeated at every `cardEdge` call site; several comments narrate the bug that *used* to be there rather than the invariant that now holds. Rule for the pass: one telling per rationale, nearest the code it protects, and a pointer elsewhere. Target ≈ 800 lines.

## E. Looks odd, is load-bearing — do **not** "simplify"

A naive tidy pass would break these. Each is documented at the site; this list exists so nobody has to rediscover them.

- `cardEdge` returning the **same style keys in both schemes** with zero values — a theme flip that adds/removes native props corrupts Android (Fabric) cards until remount.
- The **fixed 280-marker pool** and `assignSlots` in `PlacesMap` — react-native-maps 1.20 add/remove crashes iOS under the new architecture; markers are moved and hidden, never unmounted.
- The **two** `Animated.Value`s `lock` (native driver) and `ring` (JS driver) in qibla — `strokeDashoffset` cannot be native-driven, and sharing one value across drivers throws.
- `import("expo-notifications")` and `import("expo-navigation-bar")` being **lazy** — the first has a noisy module-scope side effect in Expo Go; the second resolves its native module at import and would take down `_layout.tsx` (and the error boundary) on a stale binary.
- **No native `<Modal>`** for sheets on the map screen — RN's modal host crashed on iOS while the bottom sheet was being dragged.
- `key={scheme}` on `Touchable`'s inner `Pressable` — Android's cached background drawable does not repaint on a colour-only change.
- `customMapStyle` always a **non-empty array** in both branches — a falsy style is "do nothing", not "reset".
- The Supabase read using **keyset** paging (`id > last`) rather than offsets.
- The module-level themed-style **cache** keyed on the palette object (two entries, ever).
- `I18nManager.allowRTL(false)` at **module scope** in `_layout.tsx`.
- `src/data/places.json` **never imported** by anything under `app/` or `src/` — that is the whole anti-extraction design.
- `Touchable` hoisting **layout** style keys onto its outer wrapper and applying `flexGrow` only when it did — the "Back to today chip grew 500 dp" bug is real.
- The `MAX_PLANNED = 60` notification ceiling and `IOS_PENDING_LIMIT` test.
